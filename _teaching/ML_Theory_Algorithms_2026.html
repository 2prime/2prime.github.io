1. Random vectors are nearly orthogonal in high dimensions (complete proof) 1. 高维随机向量几乎垂直（完整证明）

动机。高维随机方向的内积是大量独立、均值为零的坐标贡献之和，因而集中在零附近。

定理（Rademacher 方向）。令 \(U,V\) 独立均匀取自 \(\{-1/\sqrt d,1/\sqrt d\}^d\)。则

\[ \mathbb P(|\langle U,V\rangle|\ge\varepsilon)\le2e^{-d\varepsilon^2/2}. \]

证明。写 \(U_i=\xi_i/\sqrt d,V_i=\eta_i/\sqrt d\)，令 \(\zeta_i=\xi_i\eta_i\)。则 \(\zeta_i\) 独立且均匀取 \(\pm1\)，因此

\[ \mathbb E e^{\lambda\sum_i\zeta_i}=(\cosh\lambda)^d\le e^{d\lambda^2/2}. \]

最后一步来自 \((\log\cosh\lambda)''\le1\)。对指数变量应用 Markov，再取 \(\lambda=\varepsilon\)，得到单侧尾界 \(e^{-d\varepsilon^2/2}\)；负尾相同，两侧相加即得。

推论（同时近似正交）。若独立抽取 \(N\) 个上述方向，则

\[ \mathbb P\left(\max_{i<j}|\langle U^{(i)},U^{(j)}\rangle|\ge\varepsilon\right)\le N(N-1)e^{-d\varepsilon^2/2}. \]

证明对全部 \(\binom N2\) 对取 union bound。

球面均匀方向。对独立均匀的 \(U,V\in S^{d-1}\)，旋转不变性允许固定 \(U=e_1\)，并用 \(V=G/\|G\|\)（\(G\) 为标准高斯向量）。设 \(S=\sum_{i=2}^dG_i^2\)，由高斯尾界及 \(\mathbb E e^{-aS}=(1+2a)^{-(d-1)/2}\) 得

\[ \mathbb P(|\langle U,V\rangle|\ge\varepsilon)\le2(1-\varepsilon^2)^{(d-1)/2}\le2e^{-(d-1)\varepsilon^2/2}. \]

这里 \(0<\varepsilon<1\)。

Motivation. The inner product of high-dimensional random directions is a sum of many independent, mean-zero coordinate contributions, so it concentrates near zero.

Theorem (Rademacher directions). Let \(U,V\) be independent and uniform on \(\{-1/\sqrt d,1/\sqrt d\}^d\). Then

\[ \mathbb P(|\langle U,V\rangle|\ge\varepsilon)\le2e^{-d\varepsilon^2/2}. \]

Proof. Write \(U_i=\xi_i/\sqrt d,V_i=\eta_i/\sqrt d\), and set \(\zeta_i=\xi_i\eta_i\). The variables \(\zeta_i\) are independent and uniform on \(\{-1,1\}\), hence

\[ \mathbb E e^{\lambda\sum_i\zeta_i}=(\cosh\lambda)^d\le e^{d\lambda^2/2}. \]

The last inequality follows from \((\log\cosh\lambda)''\le1\). Apply Markov's inequality to the exponential and choose \(\lambda=\varepsilon\) to obtain the one-sided bound \(e^{-d\varepsilon^2/2}\). The lower tail is identical; adding the two bounds proves the claim.

Corollary (simultaneous near orthogonality). For \(N\) independent directions of this form,

\[ \mathbb P\left(\max_{i<j}|\langle U^{(i)},U^{(j)}\rangle|\ge\varepsilon\right)\le N(N-1)e^{-d\varepsilon^2/2}. \]

Apply the union bound to all \(\binom N2\) pairs.

Uniform directions on the sphere. For independent uniform \(U,V\in S^{d-1}\), rotational invariance allows us to fix \(U=e_1\) and write \(V=G/\|G\|\), where \(G\) is a standard Gaussian vector. Set \(S=\sum_{i=2}^dG_i^2\). The Gaussian tail bound and \(\mathbb E e^{-aS}=(1+2a)^{-(d-1)/2}\) give

\[ \mathbb P(|\langle U,V\rangle|\ge\varepsilon)\le2(1-\varepsilon^2)^{(d-1)/2}\le2e^{-(d-1)\varepsilon^2/2}. \]

Here \(0<\varepsilon<1\).

2. Random projections preserve pairwise distances (Johnson–Lindenstrauss, complete proof) 2. 随机投影保持两两距离（Johnson–Lindenstrauss，完整证明）

动机。虽然 \(k<d\) 的线性映射不能保留整个 \(\mathbb R^d\) 的所有向量长度，但可以同时保留事先固定的有限点集的两两距离。

引理（卡方集中）。若 \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_k\) 独立标准高斯，\(0<\varepsilon<1\)，则

\[ \mathbb P\left(\left|\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^kZ_i^2-1\right|\ge\varepsilon\right)\le2e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}. \]

证明。高斯积分给出 \(\mathbb Ee^{\lambda Z^2}=(1-2\lambda)^{-1/2}\)（\(\lambda<1/2\)）。上尾 Chernoff 取 \(\lambda=\varepsilon/[2(1+\varepsilon)]\)，得到

\[ e^{-k[\varepsilon-\log(1+\varepsilon)]/2}\le e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}. \]

下尾取 \(\lambda=-\varepsilon/[2(1-\varepsilon)]\)，得到

\[ e^{-k[-\varepsilon-\log(1-\varepsilon)]/2}\le e^{-k\varepsilon^2/4}. \]

合并两侧即得。

定理（Gaussian Johnson–Lindenstrauss）。给定 \(N\ge2\) 个固定点 \(x_1,\ldots,x_N\in\mathbb R^d\)，令 \(A\in\mathbb R^{k\times d}\) 的元素独立服从 \(N(0,1/k)\)。若

\[ k\ge\frac8{\varepsilon^2}\log\frac{N(N-1)}{\delta}, \]

则以至少 \(1-\delta\) 的概率，对所有 \(i,j\) 同时有

\[ (1-\varepsilon)\|x_i-x_j\|_2^2\le\|Ax_i-Ax_j\|_2^2\le(1+\varepsilon)\|x_i-x_j\|_2^2. \]

证明。对任意固定非零 \(v\)，\(Av\) 的各坐标独立且服从 \(N(0,\|v\|^2/k)\)。所以

\[ \frac{\|Av\|_2^2}{\|v\|_2^2}\stackrel d=\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^kZ_i^2. \]

由引理，每个固定向量的失败概率至多 \(2e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}\)。把 \(v\) 取为所有 \(x_i-x_j\)，对至多 \(\binom N2\) 对取 union bound，失败概率至多 \(N(N-1)e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}\le\delta\)。

量词的区别。点集必须先于 \(A\) 固定。如果看见 \(A\) 后再挑选向量，就能选到 \(\ker A\) 中的非零向量，距离保持不再成立。

Motivation. A linear map with \(k<d\) cannot preserve the lengths of every vector in \(\mathbb R^d\), but it can simultaneously preserve the pairwise distances of a fixed finite set of points.

Lemma (chi-squared concentration). If \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_k\) are independent standard Gaussian variables and \(0<\varepsilon<1\), then

\[ \mathbb P\left(\left|\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^kZ_i^2-1\right|\ge\varepsilon\right)\le2e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}. \]

Proof. Gaussian integration gives \(\mathbb Ee^{\lambda Z^2}=(1-2\lambda)^{-1/2}\) for \(\lambda<1/2\). For the upper Chernoff bound, take \(\lambda=\varepsilon/[2(1+\varepsilon)]\) to obtain

\[ e^{-k[\varepsilon-\log(1+\varepsilon)]/2}\le e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}. \]

For the lower tail, take \(\lambda=-\varepsilon/[2(1-\varepsilon)]\) to obtain

\[ e^{-k[-\varepsilon-\log(1-\varepsilon)]/2}\le e^{-k\varepsilon^2/4}. \]

Combine the two bounds.

Theorem (Gaussian Johnson–Lindenstrauss). Fix \(N\ge2\) points \(x_1,\ldots,x_N\in\mathbb R^d\), and let the entries of \(A\in\mathbb R^{k\times d}\) be independent \(N(0,1/k)\) variables. If

\[ k\ge\frac8{\varepsilon^2}\log\frac{N(N-1)}{\delta}, \]

then, with probability at least \(1-\delta\), all pairs \(i,j\) simultaneously satisfy

\[ (1-\varepsilon)\|x_i-x_j\|_2^2\le\|Ax_i-Ax_j\|_2^2\le(1+\varepsilon)\|x_i-x_j\|_2^2. \]

Proof. For each fixed nonzero \(v\), the coordinates of \(Av\) are independent \(N(0,\|v\|^2/k)\) variables. Thus

\[ \frac{\|Av\|_2^2}{\|v\|_2^2}\stackrel d=\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^kZ_i^2. \]

The lemma bounds the failure probability for each fixed vector by \(2e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}\). Apply it to all differences \(v=x_i-x_j\) and use the union bound over at most \(\binom N2\) pairs. The total failure probability is at most \(N(N-1)e^{-k\varepsilon^2/8}\le\delta\).

The order of quantifiers. The point set must be fixed before drawing \(A\). Choosing a vector after observing \(A\) can produce a nonzero vector in \(\ker A\), for which distance preservation fails.
